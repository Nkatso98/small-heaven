Discretion & Boundaries

True intimacy begins with respect.

At Small Heaven, boundaries are not restrictions—they're guarantees. They protect you, they protect us, and they preserve the sanctuary.

Our Commitments to You

  • 18+ only. Valid ID required at entry. We verify all guests.
  • Complete confidentiality. Your booking, your preferences, your presence — all confidential. We sign NDAs with staff.
  • Discrete billing. Your invoice shows generic descriptions, never specific services.
  • No photography or recording. Strictly prohibited. Violators are banned immediately.
  • Professional discretion. Our goddesses are trained professionals bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Privacy of location. We don't advertise our address publicly — confirmed guests only.

Your Responsibilities

  • Respect is non-negotiable. Required before, during, and after your visit.
  • Clear professional boundaries. Our goddesses are not for sale beyond the booked service.
  • Consent is sacred. We ask. We listen. We respect 'no' immediately.
  • Hygiene required. Guests must arrive freshly showered. This is for everyone's safety.
  • No substances. Guests must be sober upon arrival. Intoxicated guests are turned away.
  • Right to refuse service. We reserve this right for any reason, always.

Our Promise

Everything that happens inside Small Heaven is sacred, private, and protected. We take your discretion as seriously as you do. Your trust is the foundation of everything we do.

Questions about policies? Contact us on WhatsApp for discreet conversation.
